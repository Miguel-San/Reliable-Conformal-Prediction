# Figures of "Prescribing a Minimum Coverage in Split Conformal Prediction"

Notebooks that reproduce the figures of the paper by Sánchez-Domínguez, Lacasa, de Vicente, Rubio and Valero
(*Machine Learning with Applications*, under review).

| Notebook | Figures |
|---|---|
| `01_synthetic_example.ipynb` | 1, 2 and 3: synthetic example, probability of undercoverage and widths against the calibration size |
| `02_models_and_bins.ipynb` | 4 and 5: models of different complexity; input-dependent noise and calibration per regime |
| `03_public_benchmark.ipynb` | 6: UCI Airfoil Self-Noise |
| `04_critical_confidence.ipynb` | A.1: critical confidence of Appendix A |

Fig. 9 (quantile levels of both procedures against the calibration size) comes from
`../Examples/reliable_vs_classic_cp.ipynb`. Figs. 7 and 8 (industrial application) are not reproduced here, because
their data are confidential (see below).

The order-statistic indices, the achieved confidence and the guaranteed minimum coverage come from the package;
`cp_tools.py` holds the synthetic data, the laws of the scores and the Monte Carlo routines, and `cp_plot.py` the
figure style. The random streams are seeded, so the figures are the same as in the paper.

## How to run

Install the package (`pip install .` from the root of the repository), open the notebooks from this folder and run
them; the figures are saved as PNG files in `figures/`. The notebooks are stored with their outputs, so the figures can
also be seen without running them. The results of the paper were obtained with Python 3.11.13,
NumPy 2.4.4, SciPy 1.15.2, pandas 2.2.3, scikit-learn 1.6.1 and Matplotlib 3.10.1.

## Data

- Synthetic data: generated by the notebooks.
- `data/airfoil/airfoil_self_noise.dat`: Airfoil Self-Noise dataset, T. Brooks, D. Pope and M. Marcolini (1989),
  UCI Machine Learning Repository, https://doi.org/10.24432/C5VW2C, licensed under CC BY 4.0.
- Fatigue data of the industrial application: provided by Airbus Defence and Space under confidentiality, so they
  cannot be shared.
